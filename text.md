KERA where fire becomes tradition. 🔥

In Georgian, "Kera" is the heart of the home a place where people gather, stories are shared, exceptional food is prepared, and unforgettable memories are created.

That's why we chose this name.

KERA is where modern Georgian cuisine meets the rich flavors of the Caucasus. Our menu is inspired by Georgian culinary traditions, reimagined with a contemporary touch and complemented by a carefully selected collection of Azerbaijani specialties.

Our space will feature two elegant dining halls, a private VIP room, a beautiful terrace, and a welcoming veranda designed to make every guest feel at home.

Right now, we're in the most exciting part of the journey.

We're painting, building, decorating, and perfecting every detail with passion and care to create a place where tradition, flavor, and modern hospitality come together.

Every day we'll share our progress, behind-the-scenes moments, and the story of how KERA is coming to life.

Follow our journey through our stories and be part of our beginning.
